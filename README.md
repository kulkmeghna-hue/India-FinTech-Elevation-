# India FinTech Venture Research

Independent research exploring how technology, regulatory infrastructure and
digital financial rails are creating new venture opportunities in India.

## Research Areas

### 1. Payment Aggregator – Cross Border
Research into India's cross-border payment infrastructure, including:
- transaction flows
- RBI/FEMA compliance
- purpose-code classification
- merchant use cases
- competitive landscape
- operational and FX risks

### 2. InsurTech
Research into technology opportunities across:
- insurance distribution
- renewal infrastructure
- AI-enabled underwriting
- claims automation
- digital payments

### 3. AI-Assisted Investment Research
A documented workflow for using AI to:
- generate hypotheses
- map markets
- challenge investment theses
- identify contradictory evidence
- accelerate research while independently validating conclusions

## Research Philosophy

I use AI as a research accelerator, not as a substitute for judgement.

The objective is to move from:

Question → Hypothesis → Evidence → Counter-evidence → Investment View

## Disclaimer

This is an independent research project based on publicly available
information. Transaction examples are synthetic and are used only for
research and analytical purposes.
